The live story

From the underground to the road and back again.

Formed in 2010 by Joseph Pendergast, aka “Duct Tape the Clown,” Toxic Flood Waters spent five years bringing original music and circus sideshow to New Orleans and venues across the United States.

About the band

The circus is back

With an ever-changing cast of musicians and performers, the project built a bizarre, theatrical identity in the New Orleans underground before touring across the U.S.

In 2015, a near-fatal stabbing left Pendergast with nerve damage to his right arm and forced him away from music for more than a decade.

Now Toxic Flood Waters returns with Hello, a 14-track lo-fi journey recorded at Upper Nine Studios in New Orleans.
