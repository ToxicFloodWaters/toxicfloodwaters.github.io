Artist bio

Toxic Flood Waters

Formed in 2010 by New Orleans artist Joseph Pendergast, aka “Duct Tape the Clown,” Toxic Flood Waters is a collision of original music, circus sideshow, and underground performance art. With an ever-changing cast of musicians and performers, the project spent five years tearing through the New Orleans underground before taking its bizarre brand of music and spectacle across the U.S.

In 2015, a near-fatal stabbing left Pendergast with nerve damage to his right arm and forced him away from music for more than a decade. Now, the circus is back.

Toxic Flood Waters returns with Hello, a 14-track lo-fi journey recorded at Upper Nine Studios in New Orleans. Dreamy, dark, whimsical, and unmistakably New Orleans, Hello marks the beginning of a new chapter.
