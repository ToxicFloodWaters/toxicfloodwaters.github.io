Artist bio

Toxic Flood Waters

Formed in 2010 by New Orleans artist Joseph Pendergast, aka "Duct Tape Joe," Toxic Flood Waters is a collision of original music, circus sideshow, and underground performance art. After more than a decade away, the circus is back.
