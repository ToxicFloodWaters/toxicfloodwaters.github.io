Welcome back to the show

Toxic Flood Waters was formed in New Orleans in 2010 by Joseph Pendergast, aka “Duct Tape the Clown.” After a decade away from music, the project returns with Hello, a 14-track album of dreamy lo-fi sounds, dark whimsy, and unmistakably New Orleans character.

This is where release updates, tour notes, studio entries, and announcements will appear.
